Dear Juventus Academy Families,

We are excited to share some great news regarding our upcoming Juventus Academy ID Camp in Chicago on November 14–15!

Due to the exceptional interest and enthusiastic response from families, with over 120 players registered for our upcoming event, our Juventus Academy USA staff has decided to upgrade our training facilities to provide an even higher-quality experience for every participant.

Our priority is to ensure that each player has the appropriate space and environment to develop their skills, maximize their performance, and receive a thorough evaluation from our Juventus coaching staff.

For this reason, we have secured two full-size 11v11 soccer fields for Saturday, November 14, allowing us to organize age-specific technical and tactical training sessions, optimize our coaching methodology, and create the best possible environment for each player’s development and evaluation.
