Physical work, technique and games with coaches from Italy. December 19–20.

Hi Maria,

You recently looked into the Juventus ID Camp Atlanta. So you know exactly what your player will experience, this is what a day with the Juventus Academy coaches from Italy looks like.

Part 1

Physical: how they move

Movement and physical drills adapted to each age group.

Part 2

Technique: the ball, again and again

Dribbling, passing, shooting and defending. Coaches correct each player up close: groups are small, with one coach for every 12 to 15 players.

Part 3

The game: reading the match

Small-sided games to put it all into practice, in a fun and competitive environment.

Over the two days, the coaches watch every player. Standout players are noted for future Juventus Academy opportunities, like the Juventus Training Experience in Italy.
