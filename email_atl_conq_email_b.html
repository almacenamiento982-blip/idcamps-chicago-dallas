What to bring, group sizes and what happens if your player stands out.

Hi Maria,

Before finishing their registration, many families ask us the same questions. Here are the answers.

Does my player need to be on a club?

No. The ID Camp is for players of every level who want to train and be evaluated by Juventus Academy coaches.

What ages is it for?

Boys and girls 6 to 16, players and goalkeepers. Training is in groups by age.

How many players per group?

Groups are small: one coach for every 12 to 15 players, so each player gets individual attention.

What should they bring?

Soccer cleats, shin guards, a water bottle and healthy snacks. December mornings can be cold, so dress in layers.

What if my player stands out?

They are noted for future Juventus Academy opportunities, like training year-round at an Academy location or the Juventus Training Experience in Italy.

When and where?

Here are the details:

📅 Saturday and Sunday, December 19–20 · 9 AM–1 PM
📍 Rush Union Soccer Complex, 14295 Birmingham Hwy, Alpharetta, GA 30004
⚽ Boys and girls 6–16, players and goalkeepers
